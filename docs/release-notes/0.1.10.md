# Bolo 0.1.10

A normal sentence stays what you said, instead of continuing the last paste or a word list. Nothing new to set up.

## Listening

- A normal take no longer continues the last thing you pasted, or a long list of names.
- Names you set still keep that spelling. Ordinary words such as invoice, deadline, and roadmap are left alone.
- If the first listen is unsure, Bolo tries once more and keeps the clearer take. A longer made-up sentence does not win.
- A sentence is not shortened into a different one. "Scratch that", "no wait", and "I mean" still replace what you just said.
- English that was mistaken for Urdu stays English. Hindi in Devanagari still comes out as Urdu.
- Quiet speech is lifted. A click or a low hum is not turned into a word.
