# Bolo 0.1.14

A transcript Groq already returned is pasted, even when the English translation step fails.

## Dictation

- If the online service hears you, those words are inserted. A short or unclear language guess is checked once more in English, and a failed translation no longer throws the words away.
- If a take really cannot be transcribed, Bolo says "Couldn't catch that, please try again." The recording stays on this PC and is tried again when the connection is back.
- When the online service is down, Bolo uses the speech model already on the PC. If a multilingual model is needed and missing, it downloads in the background.
- The on-device speech program is checked against its published hash before it is unpacked.

## Settings

- Changes are still saved when the window closes, and the same settings come back after a restart.
