# Bolo 0.1.7

Bolo gets names and sentences right more often, and a few things you can share from Home. Nothing new to set up.

## Words

- Bolo, Kingdom Vision, and your usual names and sites are spelled the way you say them, starting the first time you open the app.
- My words is in Settings. The names you use most come first. The built-in ones stay. You can add a name or a site, and you can remove one you added.
- Spoken addresses such as "hello at name dot com" become a real email when that site is one of yours.
- If you fix the last thing you said, Bolo remembers the right spelling right away.
- Everyday grammar is tidied on your computer: capitals, periods, and small agreement fixes such as "we was" to "We were." Your meaning stays. Urdu still comes out as English.
- A status line that only said "Typed — Typed" no longer appears.

## Home

- After you have used Bolo for a while, Home can ask you to rate it in the Microsoft Store. It does not ask if you are happy first, and it does not offer a prize.
- Share Bolo copies a short note, or opens WhatsApp with it.
- At 100, 500, and 1,000 dictations, Home says thank you. One card at a time.
- Share my character saves a picture you can send.

## The app itself

- The program file is now bolo.exe. An update from 0.1.6 replaces the old copy instead of installing a second one.
- If the microphone goes quiet, Bolo tries another one that can hear.
- A dropped update check is tried again. A rejected Groq key says what went wrong.
- Tests no longer write over the Groq key on your PC.
