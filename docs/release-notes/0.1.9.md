# Bolo 0.1.9

A short phrase such as "easy ssh" stays EasySSH instead of turning into a made-up sentence. Nothing new to set up.

## Words

- Saying "easy ssh" or "EasySSH" pastes EasySSH.
- "S S H" pastes SSH.
- A real sentence such as "it's such a good idea" stays as you said it.
- A very short clip no longer continues the word list as if you had spoken those names.
- A quiet "ssh" is kept. It is not cut off as room noise.
- A short take is not rewritten into a longer sentence.
