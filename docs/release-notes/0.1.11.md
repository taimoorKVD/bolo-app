# Bolo 0.1.11

A quiet ending is more likely to stay. You can teach Bolo a spelling.

## Listening

- Letting go of the shortcut keeps a short moment of what you were still saying, so a soft last sound is less likely to disappear.
- You can teach a spelling from What Bolo has learned. It stays on this PC. Ordinary words such as invoice, there, and to are not changed in every sentence.
- Scratch that, no wait, and I mean still replace what you just said.
- Recordings are deleted after a paste unless you turn on Keep recordings on this PC.

## Your own key

- If you have a Groq key, you can save it, replace it, or add a backup. Bolo still works without one.
- A key Groq rejects is not saved. The key stays in Windows Credential Manager, not in the settings file.
